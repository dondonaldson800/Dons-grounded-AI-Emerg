3. In **Binary**, upload the `.aab` file generated via `npm run mobile:samsung:build`.
4. In **App Information**, enter Title (`Don's Grounded AI Empire`), Summary, and Full Description.
5. In **Supported Features**, mark Multi-Window and Samsung DeX as supported.
